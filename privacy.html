Privacy

Privacy information

MyLauncher is designed to minimize collection and storage of account data.

Microsoft account authentication

Authentication is performed through Microsoft's official sign-in flow in the system browser. MyLauncher does not receive or store the user's Microsoft account password.

Authentication data

Microsoft authentication cache data is stored locally using platform-protected mechanisms. Xbox, XSTS and Minecraft access credentials are used only as required for authentication and game launch and are not intentionally exposed in the user interface, documentation, or source control.

Minecraft profile data

When Minecraft Services access is available, MyLauncher may retrieve Minecraft profile information such as username, UUID, skins and capes in order to display account information and launch the game.

Local application data

MyLauncher stores launcher settings, managed Minecraft runtimes, instances, modpack metadata and integrity information on the user's own computer.

Third-party services

MyLauncher uses Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft services for account authentication and Minecraft ownership/profile verification. Those services are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Status

MyLauncher is currently under active development. This privacy information may be updated as the project evolves.
